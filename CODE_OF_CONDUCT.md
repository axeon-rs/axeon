# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Axeon to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas,
  not people.
- Assume good intent, and remember that many contributors are volunteers
  working in their spare time and in a second language.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing
  discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other channels) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainers at **[INSERT CONTACT EMAIL]**. Reports are handled confidentially,
and maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that don't follow it, and may warn, temporarily ban, or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful to the community. Responses
are proportionate to what happened.
